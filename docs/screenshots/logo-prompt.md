Design a single finished app logo mark for a product named "Mira", a voice-controlled TV
companion, presented centered on a solid flat background with generous margin on all sides
(the mark occupies about 60% of the canvas width).

STYLE
Modern flat vector logo, soft rounded geometry, premium streaming-app feel. Palette: deep
plum background #2A2140, warm coral-orange accent #F0834A fading to soft peach #F6B48F,
lavender highlight #B9A6E8, and off-white #F5F1FA for the wordmark. Subtle frosted-glass
softness on the mark, like a smooth glowing pill. Mood: calm, friendly, alive — a voice
that listens.
NO photographs, NO 3D renders, NO human faces, NO television bezel, NO robots, NO gradient
background (background must be one flat colour), NO drop shadows, NO clip-art speech
bubbles, NO stock icons.

LAYOUT
Top two-thirds: an abstract mark — a rounded soft-edged squircle in the coral-to-peach
accent, containing a simple stylised soundwave made of three vertical rounded bars of
different heights (short, tall, medium) in off-white, suggesting both a voice waveform and
a row of TV posters. A faint lavender ring hugs the squircle like a soft glow.
Bottom third: the wordmark centered beneath the mark, geometric humanist sans-serif,
lowercase, medium weight, generous letter spacing, in off-white #F5F1FA.

TEXT — SPELL EXACTLY
"mira"
NOTHING else. No tagline, no URL, no icons besides the mark, no gibberish.

FINISH & QUALITY
Crisp vector-sharp edges, perfectly centered, legible at 64px thumbnail size, high
resolution, no noise, no texture.
